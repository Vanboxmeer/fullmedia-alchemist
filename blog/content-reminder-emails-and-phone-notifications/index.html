Reminder emails and phone notifications: what we send, and when

A short email when a post is due, a nudge when drafts are waiting, and an optional morning notification on your phone. At most one email a day, and every email has a one-click unsubscribe.

The Alchemist
November 24, 2026 · 4 min read

Reminders you cannot predict get ignored. So here is exactly what the Alchemist sends, what triggers it, and how to switch it off.

The emails

Emails are sent from real events in your account, never on a timer for its own sake:

  • Welcome: once, in your first days, with the three steps to your first finished posts.
  • A post is due today: sent on a day a post is scheduled and not yet posted. It names a few of the posts so you know what to open.
  • A post is due tomorrow: a last look the day before, so you can approve it or send it back with a reason.
  • Posts are waiting: drafts have been waiting for your approval for more than a day. At most once a week.
  • Ready for its first posts: if a brand is set up but nothing has been made after a couple of days. This one is sent once.

How often

You get at most one reminder email in any 20 hours, and the same email is never sent twice. Due-today and due-tomorrow emails go out in one daily run at 13:00 UTC, so they do not trickle in through the night. If several things are due, one email covers them and the app has the per-brand detail.

Turning them off

Every reminder email has an Unsubscribe from reminders link. It asks you to confirm, then stops reminder emails. Sign-in links and receipts still arrive. Replies to these emails go to a real person.

Notifications on your phone

Add Fullmedia Alchemist to your home screen and you can turn on notifications for that device. Once a day, at 9am your local time, you get a single notification if posts are due today, past due, or only on some of your accounts, or if scheduled posts need confirming. Tap it and the app opens on the work. On a day with nothing to do, nothing is sent.

In an ordinary browser tab, the app can show a system notification for a new reminder only while the tab is open. You can turn device notifications off at any time from the bell menu, and the reminders stay in the app either way.

What is not sent

No streak guilt, no daily digest of nothing, and no automatic posting. Nothing leaves your accounts until you post it. For the full picture of the in-app side, see due, coming up and falling behind.
