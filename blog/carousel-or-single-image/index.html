Carousel or single image? How to choose for each network

A short guide to picking the format, with a source, and a way to test it on your own posts.

The Alchemist
October 30, 2026 · 4 min read

Both formats work. The question is which works where, and for whom. Start with what large studies show, then test it on your own account.

What the data says

Buffer's 2026 analysis of more than 45 million posts found that carousels lead engagement on Instagram and LinkedIn, video leads on TikTok and Pinterest, text leads on X, and images lead on Facebook. Read it here: Buffer's format study.

A simple rule of thumb

  • Teaching or listing something? Make a carousel. Each slide carries one point.
  • Announcing one thing? One image and a short caption.
  • Starting a conversation? A text post or a poll, especially on X and LinkedIn.

Test it, don't trust it

Averages hide your own audience. Post two single images and two carousels on the same account across two weeks, save the results, and open Review my top posts. The What worked where table shows the difference in views and engagement, and flags a gap of 25 percent or more. If your carousels win, make more of them. If they do not, you have saved yourself the effort.
