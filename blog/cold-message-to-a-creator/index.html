A cold message to a creator that gets a reply

Five parts, under 120 words, and one easy way out. Plus how Reach drafts it from your brand facts.

The Alchemist
October 30, 2026 · 5 min read

Creators get dozens of cold messages a week. The ones that get replies are short, specific and easy to answer. Here is a shape you can reuse.

The five parts

  1. A specific reason. Mention one real post of theirs and why it fits. "I liked your video on packing light" beats "love your content".
  2. Who you are in one line. Name, brand, what it does.
  3. One clear offer. What they would do and what they would get. State a price or product only if it is real.
  4. One easy next step. A yes or no question they can answer in a line.
  5. An easy way out. "No worries if it is not a fit." It lowers the pressure and raises replies.

What to leave out

Skip hype words, long histories, attachments on a first message, and anything you cannot back up. Skip fake urgency too.
