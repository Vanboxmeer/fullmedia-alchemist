Stay on top of every brand: due, coming up and falling behind

A reminder bell grouped by brand, a clock or a check on each brand, a calendar per brand, plus emails and phone notifications. Each reminder disappears the moment you do the thing.

The Alchemist
November 24, 2026 · 5 min read

Posting on time is mostly a memory problem. With one brand it is easy to forget a post. With five brands it is certain. The Content dashboard keeps score for you, brand by brand, and only nags about what is still undone.

The reminder bell, one list per brand

The bell in the top bar lists reminders grouped by brand. The number on it counts only things you can act on today: posts due today, posts past due, posts that went out on some accounts but not all, and scheduled posts you still need to confirm. Tap one and you land on the right brand and the right posts.

  • Due today: a post is scheduled for today and has not been posted.
  • Past due: a post is behind its date. It stays on the list while it is still relevant, up to a week after its date, then drops off.
  • Not on every account yet: the post went out on Instagram but not on LinkedIn, for example.
  • To confirm: you sent a post to your scheduler and its day has passed. Did it go out? One tap tells the Alchemist.

Two things show in the list without adding to the count, because they are not urgent: posts due tomorrow, and a brand that has had no new campaign for two weeks or more.

It clears itself

Reminders are worked out live from your posts, so they vanish as soon as the work is done. Tick the platform and the reminder is gone. Each reminder is also marked read or unread, per day, so the bell does not shout about the same thing twice.

Try it on your own brand

One brand is free, with no card. Start free.

A glance at each brand

In the brand menu, a check mark means that brand posted today. A clock means a post is due soon and nothing has gone out yet today. Each brand also has its own calendar, with every post and its campaign one click away.

Tell it where each brand posts

In Brand settings, add the accounts a brand really posts to, and only those. A post counts as fully posted once it is on all of them, or on the ones its campaign is set to. Reminders then cover only those accounts, so you are never nagged to post to a network you do not use.

Nothing posts by itself

Reminders tell you what is due. You still copy the caption, download the image and post it, or hand the queue to Claude with auto-post if you have set that up.

Beyond the app

If you are not in the dashboard, reminders can find you: a short email on the days something is due, and an optional notification on your phone. What each one sends, and when, is in reminder emails and phone notifications.

Make it a habit

Pair the bell with a short weekly check. A ten-minute weekly check-in shows how to clear what is due, approve what is waiting and start the next campaign before a brand goes quiet.
