Follow-ups that run themselves, for every brand you tick

Reach tracks who has not answered, drafts the nudge, and lets Claude send it on schedule across all your brands, up to two follow-ups per contact.

The Alchemist
November 11, 2026 · 5 min read

Most replies come after the second or third message, and most people stop after the first. With several brands the follow-ups pile up fast. Reach keeps the list so you do not have to.

What it does

When a first message has gone out and there is no reply, Reach marks the follow-up as due, with a draft ready. Claude sends the first follow-up around day 4 and a second around day 10. After two it stops, so no one is chased forever. The rules are in follow up without being annoying.

Across brands, on your terms

  • Tick the brands, then choose Follow-ups in the type filter to see exactly what is due.
  • Turn on Also select follow-ups when they are due and they are picked up as they come due. Leave it off to approve each one.
  • Each follow-up is sent from the same brand account as the original message.
Honest limit

Automation sends your approved first emails, web forms, follow-ups and replies. It never writes back on its own: when someone replies, Reach drafts an answer and Claude sends it only after you approve that draft (see approved replies). Messages on social networks and communities are also done by you.

Keep the content going too

Follow-ups are quieter than they sound. A brand that keeps posting gives a contact a reason to look back at you. Set Content to post for the same brands, and each nudge lands in front of someone who has already seen your name that week.
