Keep your accounts safe: posts checked against each platform's rules

For cannabis, vaping, alcohol, gambling, crypto, supplements and more, the Alchemist writes with the platform rules in mind and checks the result before you see it.

The Alchemist
November 1, 2026 · 5 min read

One post that breaks a platform's rules can cost you reach, and repeated ones can cost you the account. For most brands that is rare. For brands in sensitive topics it is a daily risk. So the Alchemist now looks out for it while it writes.

How it works

  • It notices the topic. From your brand profile, your campaign and your briefs, it spots topics such as cannabis, CBD and hemp, vaping and nicotine, alcohol and nightlife, gambling, crypto and investing, supplements and weight loss, firearms, and adult products. You do not switch anything on.
  • It writes to the rules. The writer is given the rules for that topic and the notes for the platforms you post to. Cannabis posts, for example, are written as education or news, never as a sale.
  • It checks the result. Selling words, medical or return claims, references to using a product, picture ideas that show use or minors, and risky hashtags are flagged. Flagged posts are rewritten, up to twice.
  • It has a last guard. Risky hashtags are removed, any sentence that still breaks a rule is dropped, and the short line the topic needs is added, such as "For adults 21+ where legal" or "Not financial advice".
  • It leaves you a note. The post's internal notes say which topic rules were applied, so you know why it reads the way it does.

What it does not do

Platforms change their rules, and moderators and automated systems do not always agree with each other. These rules are written for the strictest common ground and were last reviewed on November 1, 2026. They lower your risk. They do not remove it, they are not legal advice, and the platform's own rules page is always the final word. They cover organic posts; paid ads for these topics have extra rules and often need approval. The check reads text and picture ideas, not pictures you upload yourself. Posts made by campaigns and More posts are checked today; revising a single post and the text, thread and poll versions are not yet.

Your call stays yours

You approve every post before it goes out. Read the note, read the caption, and if a platform's rules matter to your business, check them once yourself.
