We used Reach to market Reach: 180 touches, no signups, and the new plan

Our first outreach campaign for Fullmedia Alchemist brought one reply and no signups. Here is what went wrong, what we changed, and the scoreboard we will report against, week by week.

The Alchemist
November 17, 2026 · 6 min read

The honest version first. Between 26 October and 13 November we used Reach to run outreach for Fullmedia Alchemist itself: 148 emails, 17 contact forms and 15 messages sent by hand. We got one reply, two bounces and no signups. This is what we learned, and the plan we are running now.

What the numbers said

148Emails sent
17Contact forms
1Reply
0Signups from outreach

The one reply came from a tools directory that said our product was a great fit and told us to use its listing form. That is the clue. People who list software for a living answer when you ask the question they exist to answer.

What went wrong

  • The target was too broad. Cold emails to agencies, asking for nothing specific, give a self-serve tool very little to say yes to.
  • The follow-ups did not fit. Some were written for paid creator deals, with talk of rates and lead times, and went to a software directory that had already replied.
  • We could not see clicks. No tracked links meant we could not tell whether anyone looked at a message.
  • A warm lead sat still. The listing form the directory pointed us to was not submitted for days.

What we changed

  1. Ask for something small and free. A listing, a roundup mention or a review, not a conversation.
  2. Target places built for this. Software directories, tool roundups and newsletters that take free submissions, found and checked by Reach.
  3. Tag every link. Links to our own site carry source, medium and campaign tags, so each click and signup ties back to one message (how tagged links work).
  4. Go slowly. About 10 sends a day, every draft read by a person before it goes.
  5. Never pay. Free listings only. Featured and sponsored options are skipped.

The scoreboard

By the end of November: 50 well-matched sends or submissions, at least 5 replies, at least 3 live listings and 10 signups from outreach, with the first paying customer to follow. If we land under half of that, we change the channel, not send more of the same. We will post the real numbers here, including the ones we do not like.

How you can run the same test

Everything in this campaign uses features you have: lists for coverage and partnerships, Claude automation for approved sends, web forms and an inbox sync so replies and bounces land on the right row. Start with one brand and a daily cap you are comfortable with (sending limits).
