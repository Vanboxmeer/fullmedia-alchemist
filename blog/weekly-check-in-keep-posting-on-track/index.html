A ten-minute weekly check-in to keep your posting on track

Open the bell, clear what is late, approve what is waiting, and start the next campaign before a brand goes quiet. Works for one brand or ten.

The Alchemist
November 24, 2026 · 4 min read

Consistency beats volume. A short, fixed check once a week keeps every brand moving and stops one of them quietly falling behind. Here is the routine, using what is already in the dashboard.

Minute 1: open the bell

Look at the reminders, brand by brand. Anything marked past due is the first job. If a post is no longer relevant, archive it instead of letting it sit; archiving removes it from the reminders.

Minutes 2 to 4: finish what is half done

A post that went out on some accounts but not all is the easiest win. Open it, copy the caption, post it on the missing account and tick it. If you handed posts to a scheduler, confirm which ones actually went out.

Try it on your own brand

One brand is free, with no card. Start free.

Minutes 5 to 7: approve what is waiting

Drafts that sit unapproved are the main reason a calendar goes empty. Approve the ones you like. Send the rest back with a reason, and the Alchemist redrafts them and learns what you prefer.

Minutes 8 to 10: look ahead, then refill

Open the brand calendar and check the next seven days. If a brand shows no new campaign for two weeks, start one now. On a brand with a blog, the quick start at the top of the campaigns page makes a carousel from each of your latest posts in one click. For more ideas, read post ideas for a small business and how often a small brand should post.

If you run several brands

  • Do the check brand by brand, using the bell's grouping, so a busy brand does not hide a quiet one.
  • Set the accounts each brand really posts to, so reminders never ask for a network that brand does not use.
  • Pick one fixed time. Monday morning works well, because the week's due dates are then in front of you.

Let the reminders carry the rest

Between check-ins, the bell, brand menu and calendar and the emails and phone notification catch what slips. If your week is already tight, pair this with the 20-minute social media routine.
