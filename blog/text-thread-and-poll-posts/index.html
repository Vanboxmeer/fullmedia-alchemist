Not every post is a picture: text, thread and poll versions

From any post, the Alchemist can write a text post, an X thread or a poll, fitted to each network's limits.

The Alchemist
October 30, 2026 · 4 min read

Pictures and carousels are still the core, and they already cover Instagram, TikTok, Reddit, Facebook and LinkedIn. But some networks reward plain words. Open a post's menu and choose Other formats.

What you can make

FormatAccountsLimits built in
Text postX, Threads, LinkedIn, Facebook, RedditX 280 characters; Reddit title 120
ThreadX, Threads3 to 6 posts, each 280 or fewer
PollX, LinkedInQuestion 140; 2 to 4 options of 25 (X) or 30 (LinkedIn)

How it works

  1. Open the post menu and pick Other formats.
  2. Choose the format and the account. A tick shows the accounts your brand already has.
  3. Press Write it. It costs 1 credit, and the credit comes back if the write fails.
  4. Check the character counts, then copy it into the network.

The new version uses only the post's caption and your brand details. It is told never to invent offers, numbers or dates. It writes text only: nothing is posted for you and no picture is made. Earlier results for the post reload when you open the sheet.

Why bother

Buffer's 2026 analysis found text posts lead on X. Matching the format to the network is a quick win you can get from a post you already approved.
