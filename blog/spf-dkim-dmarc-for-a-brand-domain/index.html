SPF, DKIM and DMARC for a brand domain: GoDaddy, Vercel, Cloudflare

Three DNS records stop outreach landing in spam. What each one is, the exact value to add, and how to find the right place to add it, whichever company hosts your DNS.

The Alchemist
November 17, 2026 · 7 min read

Mail from a domain that has never sent anything, with no authentication, is likely to land in spam. Three DNS records fix most of that. They take about ten minutes per domain, and they do the same job on every host: only the screen differs.

Find where your DNS lives first

DNS lives wherever the domain's nameservers point, which is not always where you bought the domain. A domain bought at GoDaddy can have its nameservers at Vercel or Cloudflare. If a record you add does nothing, check the nameservers. Add records only at the host that the nameservers name.

The three records

  • SPF says who may send for the domain. One TXT record at host @. For Google Workspace: v=spf1 include:_spf.google.com ~all. A domain may have only one SPF record, so merge into an existing one.
  • DKIM signs each message. In Google Admin open Apps, Google Workspace, Gmail, Authenticate email, pick the domain, and generate the record. It is a TXT record at the host Google shows (usually google._domainkey). Add it, wait, then press Start authentication.
  • DMARC tells receivers what to do when a check fails. A TXT record at host _dmarc: v=DMARC1; p=none; rua=mailto:you@yourcompany.com. Start with p=none and tighten later.

Adding a TXT record on each host

  • GoDaddy: My Products, the domain, DNS. Add New Record, type TXT, enter the name and value, Save. Leave the name as @ for the domain itself. Some GoDaddy screens ask for only the part before the domain, so enter google._domainkey, not the full name.
  • Vercel: Domains, the domain, DNS records, add a TXT record with the name and value. This only works when Vercel's nameservers are the domain's nameservers.
  • Cloudflare: the domain, DNS, Records, Add record, type TXT. TXT records are not proxied, so there is nothing to turn off.
  • Namecheap: Domain List, Manage, Advanced DNS, Add New Record, type TXT. Namecheap shows the host as a short name too, so leave the domain off.
Two mistakes to avoid

Two SPF records on one domain breaks SPF, so merge instead of adding a second. And never paste a DKIM value split across two lines: a stray space or line break fails the check.

Check it worked

Records usually show within minutes, and can take up to 48 hours. In Google Admin the DKIM screen says when authentication is on. Send yourself a message, open it in Gmail, choose Show original, and look for SPF, DKIM and DMARC all marked PASS.
