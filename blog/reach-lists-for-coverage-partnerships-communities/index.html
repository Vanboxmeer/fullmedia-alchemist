Build your target list in minutes: coverage, partners and communities

Tell Reach who you want to reach. It researches them, checks the rules, and hands back a ranked list with a first draft for each. You accept or reject every row.

The Alchemist
November 10, 2026 · 5 min read

The slowest part of outreach is not writing the message. It is building the list: finding the right people, finding a real way to contact them, and checking that you are allowed to post there. Reach does that research for you and keeps you in charge of what goes in.

Three kinds of list

CoverageCreators, outlets, newsletters, podcasts and channels that could feature your brand
PartnersCompanies and agencies to introduce a brand or a service to
CommunitiesFree places to post: subreddits, forums, Discords, guest posts, directories

For communities, Reach reads each place's rules first, tags the result (open, promo thread only, ask the moderators first, submission page, banned) and drops anywhere that bans promotion. The full story is in free communities and guest posts.

How a run works

  1. Open Find in the Workbench, pick the kind of list and how many results you want, and add any criteria: niche, language, contact route, places to avoid.
  2. Reach researches the web. You can also queue the same job as a request and let your own Claude agent do the research, then come back to review.
  3. Every find arrives as a Proposed row with a verified way to reach them (email, contact form, profile link or community page), where that was found, and a first-draft message written from your brand facts.
  4. Accept the rows you like and reject the rest. Accepted rows join your list ranked by priority score.
Honest limits

Reach never invents a contact or a price. A row with no verifiable way to reach it is not added. On the Free plan a run returns up to 3 results. Reach finds people and keeps you organised, it does not vouch for them, so read verify before you pay or share before you spend anything.

What it costs

Reach has a free plan with 10 AI drafts a month. Paid plans start at $29 a month (Starter), with Growth at $79 for up to 3 brands and Agency at $249 for up to 10. Finding and drafting use your plan's AI calls.

Give the list something to land on

A list is only as good as what people find when they check you out. The Alchemist in Content reads your website and writes and designs a steady stream of posts in your own style, so the people you contact find an active brand.
