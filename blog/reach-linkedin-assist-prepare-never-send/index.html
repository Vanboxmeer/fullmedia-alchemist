LinkedIn assist: Claude prepares, you press Send

Claude can open your signed-in LinkedIn, check it is the right profile, and put your message in the box. It never presses Send. You do.

The Alchemist
November 17, 2026 · 5 min read

LinkedIn does not allow automated messaging, so Reach does not automate it. LinkedIn assist takes the tedious part only: Claude finds the person, opens the message box and puts your approved text in it. You read it and press Send yourself.

How it works

  • Claude opens your own LinkedIn in your own browser and checks it is the profile the brand uses before doing anything.
  • For each task it places the text in the message or connection-note box. It never presses Send.
  • It can read your conversations, read only, so Reach can draft replies to what people wrote back.
  • If the profile cannot be confirmed, or LinkedIn shows any warning, it stops and turns the feature off.

Set it up

  1. Save a LinkedIn profile for the brand under Brand, Sender & signature (or set a workspace default).
  2. In Automation, tick the brand and open LinkedIn assist. Turn it on.
  3. Set the most to prepare a day and the pause between actions. Keep both low.
  4. Ask Claude: Check my Alchemist Reach tasks

Bring in people you already know

Reach can also import your recent conversations and, if you choose, your newest connections. Claude reads them and each person's public profile and sends nothing. You then accept or decline each person, and Reach drafts for the ones you keep.

Keep your account safe

Go slowly. A handful a day from a real profile you use is fine. Bursts of messages are what get accounts restricted. See protecting your accounts.
