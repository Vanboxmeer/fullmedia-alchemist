Who to pitch first: Reach now ranks every prospect

A 0 to 100 priority score on every row, a sortable column, and a Top 10 tick box. No AI calls, nothing sent.

The Alchemist
October 30, 2026 · 4 min read

A list of 200 creators is not a plan. You need to know which ten to write to this week. Reach now scores every open row from what is already on it, and shows the best ten with one tick.

How the score works

Every row gets a number from 0 to 100, built from five things that are already in the row:

30Audience: followers on a log scale, or site authority
25Fit: your own 1 to 10 fit score
25Reachable: an email beats a message link, which beats a form
10Proof and price known
10Where the deal stands

Won, lost, on hold and do-not-contact rows are left out, so the ranking only shows people you could still write to. It works the same on a list you found with Find, imported, or typed in by hand.

Where to see it

  1. Open the Workbench. There is a new, sortable Priority column. Green is 75 and up, amber 55 to 74, grey below that. Hover a pill to see its breakdown.
  2. Tick Top 10 prospects to show only the ten best open rows.
  3. Work down the list: draft, review, send from your own Gmail, mark sent.
Honest limit

The score does not measure engagement yet, because rows have no engagement field. A big account with a dead audience can still score high, so use your fit score to correct it.

The score is pure ordering. It costs no AI calls and sends nothing. Reach never sends a message for you.
