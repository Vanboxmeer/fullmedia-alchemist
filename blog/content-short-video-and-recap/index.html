Posts that move: video-ready slides, short video and recaps

Pick Post, Prepared for video or Short video when you create. The Alchemist builds vertical slides, or the video itself, in your own browser.

The Alchemist
November 10, 2026 · 4 min read

Reels, Shorts and TikTok want a vertical video, and most small teams do not have an editor. When you create posts in Content there is now a choice of output under Single, Carousel and Mixed.

Three outputs

PostThe usual card or carousel
Prepared for video9:16 slides with empty slots for text, stickers and timing. Every plan, no credits
Short videoThe finished video, 1 extra credit per post. Paid plans

Prepared for video gives you a clean version and a version with dashed slots and keep-clear zones for the app's own buttons, downloaded as a zip with a slot sheet, so you can finish it in any editor.

Short video uses the plan the Alchemist writes for the post: your brand colour, typeface and logo, the post's pictures, the timing, text and emoji stickers, with a fade, push or cut between scenes. It is recorded in your browser and downloaded, never stored. There is no sound.

Recaps

A recap post, a roundup of earlier posts, now makes its video from those posts' own pictures: the recap headline over the first picture, then each source post in quick cuts with its headline, ending on the closing card with the QR and call to action. It costs no extra credit.

Try it

  1. Start a campaign or open Create posts.
  2. Under the Single, Carousel and Mixed buttons, choose the output.
  3. Approve the post, then use its menu to prepare or make the video.
Honest limits

Videos are silent, so add music in the app you post from. Short video needs a paid plan.
