Close the tab, keep the posts: background generation

Your phone sleeps, the connection drops, the batch carries on. And if it fails, your credits come back.

The Alchemist
October 30, 2026 · 3 min read

On a phone, tapping Transmute and watching the screen go dark used to mean the batch could be lost. Generation now runs in the background on our side, so a sleeping phone no longer kills it.

What changed

  • Transmute starts a job and returns at once. The page checks progress every few seconds.
  • Lock the screen or switch apps. When you come back, or reload, the page picks the job up and finishes the normal steps.
  • If the engine fails and no posts were made, the credits are refunded. If it fails quickly, it tries once more.
  • Tap Transmute twice by accident and the second tap joins the job that is already running, so you are not charged twice.
  • A job that gets stuck for more than nine minutes is closed and refunded.
Equivalent exchange

Nothing is taken without something to show for it. A failed batch returns its credits, and you can see the refund in your usage.

There is a fair-use limit of 30 starts an hour. Credits and plan limits work exactly as before.
