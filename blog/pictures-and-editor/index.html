A picture for every post, and an editor to perfect it

Every post now gets its own AI-generated scene image — cinematic, photoreal, watercolor, or any of a dozen styles. And when it's close but not quite right, the editor lets you tweak it without starting over.

The Alchemist
October 17, 2026 · 4 min read · updated October 30

Social posts with pictures get more engagement. That's not a secret. But making a unique image for every post, every day, is a full-time job. The Alchemist now does it for you — one credit per picture, in the style that matches your brand.

One credit, one scene

When you create a campaign or generate posts, each one gets its own picture. Not a stock photo. Not a template with your logo stamped on it. A scene built from your caption, your brand's look, and the art style you pick. If your post is about a fall recipe launch, the picture is a fall kitchen scene. If it's about a new podcast episode, the picture matches that story.

Each picture costs one credit from your plan — the same credit you'd spend on the caption itself. No separate image add-on, no surprise charges.

Art styles that fit your brand

Not every brand wants the same look. A law firm posting thought-leadership content doesn't want watercolors. A children's bookshop doesn't want corporate photography. The Alchemist offers a range of art styles:

  • Cinematic — dramatic lighting, wide aspect, film-grain texture
  • Photoreal — clean, modern, looks like a product shoot
  • Watercolor — soft washes, hand-painted warmth
  • Illustration — bold outlines, flat color, editorial feel
  • Character scene — your brand's character or mascot in a setting

Pick one in your campaign settings, or let the Alchemist choose based on your brand profile. If you switch styles later, existing pictures stay — only new ones pick up the change.

Carousels: 2–4 slides, each one different

Carousel posts get multiple slides, each with its own image. Slide 1 is the hook — the attention-grabbing scene. Slides 2 through 4 carry the detail: a close-up, a list, a behind-the-scenes angle. Every slide is rendered individually, so they tell a visual story instead of repeating the same shot with different text.

The editor: fix it without starting over

Sometimes a picture is 90% right. The layout is good, the scene is right, but the headline is a word too long or the logo is in the wrong corner. That's what the editor is for.

Open any post's picture and tap Edit. You can adjust the headline text, move the logo, swap the accent color, or change the layout — without regenerating the entire image. The editor tracks which parts you've touched, so if you redo the campaign later your manual edits stay saved.

From the workshop

The editor uses the same rendering engine as the original picture. What you see in the editor is exactly what gets downloaded or posted — no export surprises.

SEO-friendly from the start

Every generated image gets a real file name and a real alt-text description. Not IMG_3847.png, not untitled-design.jpg. A post about weekend brunch recipes produces an image named weekend-brunch-recipes-scene.png with alt text that describes what's in the picture. Search engines read those. Screen readers read those. It costs you nothing extra.

Download and go

Every picture has a download button right on the post card. Tap it, get a full-resolution PNG. No watermark, no "upgrade to download" gate. If your plan includes the credit to make the picture, you own the picture.
