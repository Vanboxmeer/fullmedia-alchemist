Your account, locked down: two-step sign-in, passwords and your data

Reset a password, add an authenticator app, download everything we hold, or ask us to delete your account.

The Alchemist
October 30, 2026 · 3 min read

A tool that holds your brand and your outreach should be easy to secure. Open the account menu and choose Password & security.

What is there

  • Forgot your password? On the sign-in page. The reply never says whether an account exists, and the reset email brings you back to a "choose a new password" screen.
  • Two-step sign-in (optional). Use an authenticator app. Scan a QR code, type the six-digit code once, and from then on the dashboard, Reach and the report pages ask for a code at sign-in. You can turn it off, which needs a fresh code.
  • Sign out everywhere. Ends every session on every device.
  • Download my data. A file with your workspace, billing and usage records. Payment IDs, tokens and keys are left out. It covers workspaces you own; brands you were invited to appear by name only.
  • Delete my account. This files a request with support. Nothing is deleted automatically, and the team confirms with you by email first.
Honest limit

The code check guards the screens when you sign in. It does not stop someone who already holds a stolen session token, so sign out everywhere if a device is lost.
