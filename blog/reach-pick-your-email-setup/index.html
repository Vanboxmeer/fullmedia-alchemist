Pick your email setup: solo, agency or one per client

Five ways to send outreach from Reach, what each costs, and which login each one needs. Start here, then follow the setup guide for the one you choose.

The Alchemist
November 17, 2026 · 5 min read

Before you connect anything, decide where your messages should come from. The answer depends on how many brands you run, how much brand identity matters, and whether Claude or you will press send. This page puts every option side by side.

The options

  • One mailbox, everything automated. Cheapest and quickest. Every message comes from one address, so put the brand in the first line and the signature. Good for a trial or a single brand. Setup.
  • One mailbox per brand, automated. Each brand sends from its own address and keeps its own sending reputation. Costs a mailbox (a Workspace seat or a Gmail account) per brand. Best for agencies. Setup.
  • Aliases, sent by hand. One login, many brand addresses, you press send in Gmail. Good for a few high-value messages and for replies. Setup.
  • Reach drafts, you send. No connector at all. Reach opens the draft in Gmail and you send it. Slowest, fully in your hands.
  • Web forms and LinkedIn alongside email. Claude can submit contact forms with your browser and prepare LinkedIn messages without sending them (LinkedIn assist).
The one rule to remember

Automated mail leaves from the Google account Claude's Gmail connection is signed in to. It does not use aliases. The full explanation is in how to send as each brand.

Which one are you?

  • Solo, one or two brands: one mailbox, brand in the signature, aliases for hand-sent replies.
  • Freelancer with a handful of clients: one mailbox per client who cares how the email looks, one shared mailbox for the rest.
  • Agency: one mailbox per client, a low daily cap on each, and the inbox sync so replies and bounces show up on the right row.

Whatever you pick

  • Authenticate each sending domain first: SPF, DKIM and DMARC.
  • Start at about 10 sends a day and raise it slowly (sending limits).
  • Approve every row yourself. Nothing unapproved is ever sent.

Choose your setup, then start small.
