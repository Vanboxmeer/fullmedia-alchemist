Approved posts, posted by your own Claude

Claude opens your own accounts in Chrome and posts what you approved, with a daily cap, a pause between posts and a live link as proof.

The Alchemist
November 10, 2026 · 5 min read

You approved the posts. Now somebody has to open four apps, paste four captions and attach the right pictures every day. Auto-post with Claude does that part, in your own browser, signed in as you.

How it works

  1. In a campaign menu choose Auto-post with Claude and turn it on for the brand.
  2. Approve posts as usual. The queue holds the exact caption for each account, the pictures and the date.
  3. Claude picks up one post at a time, opens the network's composer in Chrome, pastes the exact text, attaches the pictures, posts, and copies the live link back.
  4. The link lands in the post's Publishing Status so you can see what went out and where.

It covers Instagram, Facebook, X and LinkedIn. TikTok and Pinterest stay manual. If you already schedule with Metricool you can use that connector instead.

Safeguards

AccountClaude checks the signed-in account matches the one you saved for the brand, or it stops and pauses
CapA daily limit (default 5) and a pause between posts (default 2 minutes)
ProofA post counts as posted only when Claude returns the live link

It never logs in for you, solves CAPTCHAs or deletes anything, and it checks for a duplicate before posting.

Honest limits

Posting through a website can run against a network's terms and can trigger account checks, uploads can fail, and a post cannot be undone from here. Start with a small cap and watch the first few.
