One idea, many networks: reshape it, don't copy-paste it

Why the same caption on every network underperforms, and how to adapt one idea in five minutes.

The Alchemist
October 30, 2026 · 5 min read

Copying one caption to every network is the most common social media shortcut, and the most visible. Each network has its own length, link rules and habits. A post that is right on LinkedIn reads wrong on X.

What changes from network to network

NetworkFit it for
X280 characters on a free account, so lead with the point; 2 tags at most
LinkedInUp to 3,000 characters; a story or lesson works; 3 tags
InstagramImage first; Instagram announced a cap of 5 hashtags; links in captions are not tappable
TikTok and PinterestLinks in captions are not tappable; the first line must hook
FacebookLonger is fine; links are inline; 3 tags

The five-minute method

  1. Write the idea as one sentence: who it is for and what they get.
  2. Choose the format per network: carousel on Instagram and LinkedIn, text on X, a picture on Facebook.
  3. Change the first line for each network. The first line is the post.
  4. Move the link to where it works: inline on X, LinkedIn and Facebook; in the bio or a comment elsewhere.
  5. Trim the tags to the number that network can use.
