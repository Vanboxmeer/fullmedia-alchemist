Alcohol, gambling, crypto, supplements: the posting rules that matter

Each topic has one or two mistakes that cause most of the trouble. Here is what to avoid and what to say instead.

The Alchemist
November 1, 2026 · 5 min read

Sensitive topics are not off limits. They need a little more care, and for most of them the same few habits keep you out of trouble. Paid ads for these topics also often need licences or approval from the platform, which is a separate process from posting.

Alcohol and nightlife

Aim at adults of legal drinking age and keep minors out of the picture entirely. Do not encourage heavy or fast drinking, getting drunk, or drinking and driving, and do not claim health benefits. Talk about the place, the craft and the people. End with "Please drink responsibly. 21+."

Gambling and betting

Never promise wins or "easy money", never call it risk-free, and never suggest it fixes money problems. It is entertainment for adults. Do not state bonuses or odds that you cannot back up exactly. End with "21+. Please gamble responsibly."

Crypto, trading and investing

No promised returns, no "100x", no "to the moon", no pressure to buy now, and no telling people to hold a named coin. Educate, explain and say plainly that prices can fall. End with "Not financial advice."

Supplements and weight loss

Never say a product cures, treats or prevents a disease. No before-and-after bodies, no promised pounds lost, no "miracle" or "detox" language. Use only the benefits you can state, worded as general wellness support. End with "Not medical advice."

Firearms and adult products

Firearms: safety, law and sport only, never a sale arranged in a post. Adult products: nothing explicit, nothing sexualised, adults only.
