How it works

From a request to a closed deal.

Finding the right options is the slow part, so that is where Reach starts. The rest of the work stays in one place after that.

Ask

Say who you want: channel types, niche, audience size, language, free or paid, and a budget cap per placement.

Review the shortlist

Matches arrive as Proposed rows with a reason each one fits, a verified contact route and a source, and a first draft.

Send

Open the draft in Gmail with your signature or copy it, then mark it sent. You approve every message.

Track and close

Replies land on the same deal. Reach shows who owes the next move, nudges stalled deals and holds the approvals checklist.

The biggest time saver

Ask for a shortlist. Get one back.

Instead of hunting through channels and About pages, you describe the audience. Reach does the research with your connected web research and Gmail (read-only) and brings the matches to you.

  • One request, many channels. YouTube, Twitch, newsletters, sites, podcasts, communities and tip lines are all on the list.
  • Your limits are the filter. Audience range, language, free or paid, and a budget cap per placement.
  • A verified contact or it does not count. Each match carries a contact route and where it was found.
  • A reason it fits. Every proposed row says why, so you accept or reject in seconds.
  • A first draft each. Written from your brand facts, ready to review.

Approvals

Do not move on until it is signed.

Some deals need an NDA, a signature, or a yes from the client, licensing or payments before they go further. Set the checks once per brand and Reach makes sure they are confirmed at the right stage.

Pick your checks

NDA signed, contract signed, client approval, licensing cleared, payment approved, legal review, right materials shared. Or write your own.

Reminder or Required

A Reminder is one click to continue. Required means you must record a reason to go on. Choose the stage each applies to.

Everything is logged

Every confirmation and every override is written on the deal, so you can see who agreed to what and when.

FAQ

Before you choose.

Where do the options come from?

From you or from a request. You can add them yourself, paste or import a deck or sheet, take them from the public library other Reach users share, or ask Reach to find more. A request runs the in-app agent with your connected web research, adds matches as Proposed rows with a reason each one fits, and drafts a first message for you to review.

Will Reach make up contacts or prices?

No. A request only returns options that have a contact route and a recorded source, and prices shown are only what is on file. Where nothing is on file, the cell stays empty for you to fill.

Does Reach send emails for me?

No. Reach drafts the message and you review it. You open it in Gmail with your signature, or copy it, and then mark it sent. Nothing leaves without you.

What happens when someone replies?

The reply goes on the same deal with its history. Reach shows who owes the next move, how long it has been waiting, and prepares a nudge when a deal stalls.

What is an AI call?

The drafts, research and assistant answers Reach does for you. Each plan has a monthly allowance and a usage meter in your workspace settings, so you always see what is left.

How does the bundle work?

Put Content and Reach on the same tier and you pay one price for both, 10% below buying them separately, on one invoice. Different tiers are billed separately with no discount. You can start either tool free and add the other later.

Can my client see the deals?

On Growth and Agency, yes, without seeing your internal notes. Growth gets client review links, Agency gets client portals. The client view shows the message, the price you choose to show, and basic facts, never creator contacts, notes or your quotes.
