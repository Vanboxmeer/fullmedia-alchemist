FAQ

Before you choose.

Do I have to pay to start?

No. Both tools start free with no card. Content Free gives you 1 brand and 30 credits a month. Reach Free gives you 1 brand and 150 AI credits to try. You only see a plan page when you hit a limit and want more.

Do I need both Content and Reach?

No. They are two tools with separate plans and one login. Many people use only one. If you later want both, put them on the same tier and pay 10% less than buying them separately, on one invoice.

How does the bundle work?

Choose the same tier of Content and Reach, for example Growth on both. You pay one price on one invoice, 10% below buying them separately. Different tiers, or Free on one tool, are billed separately with no discount. You can start either tool free and add the other later.

What is a credit?

Content uses credits as its currency. One image post is 1 credit and a 3-slide carousel is 3 credits. You see the price before you press Transmute, and if an image fails its credits come straight back.

Will it make up facts about my brand?

No. The Alchemist reads your own website and brand material. Anything your site does not say stays blank for you to fill, figures and quotes are checked, and dates come only from you.

Does Reach send emails for me?

Not on its own. Reach drafts the message and you review and approve it. You can send it yourself, or switch on Automation so your own Claude sends only the messages you approved. Nothing leaves without your approval.

What are the Claude connectors?

Right now there is one for Reach: Alchemist Reach, which sends your approved emails and fills your approved forms. It is a private link you make inside the app (Automation in Reach) and add to your own Claude as a custom connector. It runs in your own Claude, so it uses your Claude plan. A Content connector that posts your approved posts is coming back soon.

Do I need a connector for every brand?

No. One Reach link covers all your brands. Which brands are switched on, and which accounts or mailboxes each one uses, are settings inside the app. You can pause a single brand without touching the others.

What do I set in Claude after adding a connector?

Open the connector in Claude and set its 3 tools to Always allow (the check icon, or the “Needs approval” menu at the top of the tool list). If you leave them on Needs approval, Claude stops and asks you before every step, so it cannot work through your queue. Always allow only lets Claude use those tools. It still can only handle items you approved in the app.

Is the connector link safe to share?

No, treat it like a password. It is shown once. Anyone who has it could hand out and report on your approved items. Never post it or screenshot it. If it leaks, remove it in the app and make a new one. Claude never logs in for you, types passwords or solves CAPTCHAs, and it stops and tells you if it finds the wrong account signed in.

Do I need Metricool?

No. Content prepares the copy and images for you to post by hand, or you can send them to Metricool if you already schedule there. Automatic posting through your own Claude is coming back soon.

Can I cancel or change plan later?

Yes. You manage your plan, card and cancellation inside the app, and each tool has its own plan.
