Questions

Questions worth asking first.

Does it just make things up about my brand?

No, and that's the rule it's built around. It only uses what your website actually says, and anything your page doesn't say is left blank rather than guessed at, because an invented detail would become the grounding for every post after it. You can see and edit everything it learned.

Do I need any design skills?

No. Every post arrives designed, with branded artwork, a headline and a working QR code. You can edit the caption and hashtags yourself, or send a post back with a note and it redrafts.

What if I don't like a post?

Send it back with a reason and it redrafts. Every post gets free redos: 1 on Free, 2 on Starter, 3 on Growth, 5 on Agency. After that, a redo costs credits like a new post.

How does it get published?

You post it yourself, wherever you already do. Copy the caption and hashtags in one click, download the images, then tick where it went out. There’s no direct connection to a scheduler yet.

Can I run client brands on one account?

Yes. Growth covers up to 3 brands and Agency up to 10, each with its own look, voice and knowledge. Need more than 10? Email brad@unitedpress.media.

What is a credit?

One credit buys one slide on the Fast model; Pro costs 2 per slide and Max costs 4. A single-image post on Fast costs 1 and a 3-slide carousel costs 3. A new picture of your character adds 2. You see the cost before you create anything, and if an image or character picture fails, its credits are returned automatically.

What about video?

AI video is coming soon. It isn't available on any plan yet; Growth and Agency will include it when it launches.
