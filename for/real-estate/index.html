Who it’s for

Social posts for real estate agents

Paste your listing pages and get one finished post per property, using only what the listing says.

Every new listing deserves a post, and every open house needs a reminder. The Alchemist turns each listing page into a finished post in your agency's look, with a QR code straight to the listing.

How it works for an agent

  1. Paste your agency website once so the Alchemist learns your look, logo and voice.
  2. Add one link per listing. In "one post per event, product or link", paste each listing page and add the open-house date if there is one.
  3. Approve and post. Each post points to its own listing with a QR code.
Facts from the listing only

Bedrooms, prices and features only appear if the listing page states them, and any number is checked against that page in code. Open-house dates come only from you.

Good to know

  • Before you approve, each post is checked for platform limits and whether its link opens.
  • Posts can go into your calendar, with a reminder email on the day.
  • Running a team or several offices? Growth covers up to 3 brands, each with its own look.

Questions

Does it make up property details?

No. It writes only from the listing page and your brief, and any number on a post has to appear there. If the page doesn't say it, the post leaves it out.
