Who it’s for

Social posts for cafés and restaurants

Menu launches, specials, events and opening hours, turned into finished posts from your own website. You approve; you stay in the kitchen.

A café has plenty to say: a new menu, a weekend special, live music on Friday, longer summer hours. What it doesn't have is time to design posts. The Alchemist reads your website, learns your look and voice, and turns each of those into a finished post.

How it works for a café

  1. Paste your website. Your colours, logo, typefaces and the way you write are picked up in one click.
  2. List what's coming up. Use "one post per event, product or link": the new menu, the Friday gig, the brunch launch. Add real dates and links.
  3. Approve and post. Each post has a caption, 4–5 hashtags and an image with a QR code to your menu or booking page.
Only true things

The Alchemist never invents a price, a date or a dish. Dates come only from you, and any number on a post has to appear on your site or in your brief.

What you get

  • Posts in your own colours and typefaces, not a template everyone else uses.
  • Your mascot, if you have one, in new scenes for each post.
  • Every post's date in your calendar, and a reminder email the day it's due.

A café with one location fits the Free plan to start (30 credits a month, no card) and Starter ($25/mo, 300 credits) for posting every day.
