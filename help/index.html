Report a problem

Open your account menu in Content or Reach and choose Report a problem. Add what you expected and what happened.

Plans and billing

Change plan, update your card or cancel from Plans & billing in your account menu. Each tool has its own plan.

Verify before you trust

Reach does not verify partners or library sellers. Read how to check them before you pay or share anything sensitive.

Claude connectors

Two private links, one for Content and one for Reach, let your own Claude post or send what you approved. Set each connector’s 3 tools to Always allow. See the FAQ.

Credits

One image post is 1 credit and a 3-slide carousel is 3. Failed images are refunded. See the FAQ.

Use the same method you signed up with. Signing out of one tool signs you out of both.
