About

Who is behind Fullmedia Alchemist

A small product from United Press Media, built first for our own client work and then opened to everyone.

Fullmedia Alchemist turns a website into finished social media posts, and Alchemist Reach helps you find the right people to tell about your brand and draft the messages. Both are made by United Press Media.

Who we are

I'm Brad, the founder. United Press Media is a small media and outreach company, and we built these tools because we needed them: writing and designing posts for several brands, and running outreach for them, without losing track of who we had written to or what was true about each brand. We used the tools on our own clients before we opened them to other people.

What we make

Fullmedia Alchemist (Content) reads your site once, learns the brand, and drafts posts with images that use only facts you or your site provide. Alchemist Reach finds and ranks contacts, drafts each message from your brand facts, and keeps replies in one place. Nothing is sent without a person approving it. You can see current plans and the launch offer on the pricing page.

How the Journal is written

The Journal is where we share playbooks and what we learn running our own outreach. We use Claude to help draft and edit articles, and we check them against what the product really does. Where we quote numbers from our own campaigns, they come from our own records, including the ones that didn't go well, such as our first run of 148 emails that produced one reply. We update an article's date when we change it. If you spot something wrong, please tell us.
