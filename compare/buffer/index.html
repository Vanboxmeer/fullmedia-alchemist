Fullmedia Alchemist and Buffer

Not rivals: one makes the posts, the other schedules them. Here is how they fit together.

Buffer is a social media scheduler: you connect your channels, queue posts and see analytics. Fullmedia Alchemist makes the posts: it reads your website and writes and designs finished ones. They do different jobs, so they work well together.

Using them together

  1. Make the posts in the Alchemist. Approve the ones you like; send the rest back with a note.
  2. Copy the caption and download the image. One click each.
  3. Queue it in Buffer for the day the Alchemist scheduled it.
  4. Tick where it went in the Alchemist and paste the live link, so it learns what works and your streak counts.
Why not one tool?

Making a good post and publishing it on time are different jobs. The Alchemist puts its effort into the first, and says so: it does not connect to your social accounts.

Questions

Does Fullmedia Alchemist work with Buffer?

Yes, alongside it. Make and approve posts in the Alchemist, then copy the caption and download the image into Buffer. There is no direct connection between them yet.
