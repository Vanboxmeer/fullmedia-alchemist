Fullmedia Alchemist vs Canva

Canva is a design editor you work in. The Alchemist reads your website and hands you finished posts. Here is how they differ, and when to use which.

Both help you end up with social posts that look good. The difference is who does the work. In Canva you design each post yourself, from templates, with AI helpers. In Fullmedia Alchemist you paste your website once, say what to post about in a sentence, and the Alchemist writes and designs the posts for you.

Choose Canva if

  • You enjoy designing and want full control over every pixel.
  • You need print, presentations or video editing as well as social posts.
  • You already have a writer and just need a design tool.

Choose the Alchemist if

  • You'd rather approve posts than make them. A campaign of finished posts takes about a minute.
  • You want posts that only state true things about your business. If your site doesn't say it, the Alchemist leaves it out.
  • You run several brands and need each one to keep its own look and voice.

Use both

Plenty of people do. Let the Alchemist write and design the regular posts, download the image, and open it in Canva when a special post deserves a hand-made touch.

Honest limits

The Alchemist does not post for you or schedule posts, and video is still coming soon. Canva covers far more kinds of design. The Alchemist does one job: turning what your business actually says into finished social posts.

Questions

Is Fullmedia Alchemist a Canva alternative?

For social posts, yes: it writes and designs finished posts from your website, so you approve instead of design. For print, presentations or video editing, Canva does far more.

Can I edit Alchemist posts in Canva?

Yes. Download the image and open it in Canva or any editor. You can also edit the caption and hashtags in the Alchemist, or send a post back with a note and it redrafts.
